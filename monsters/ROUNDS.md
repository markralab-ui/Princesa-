# Myth Monster Freeze: the six rounds

Every one of the world's 195 countries (the 193 UN members plus Vatican City and Palestine) gets at least one monster from its own myths and legends over six rounds. Each round has all six continents, so every round can be played on its own. Pick any round from the start screen; the world map in the Treasure Book lights up each country whose monster you have frozen, in every round.

Continents with few countries (South America, Oceania, North America) also get extra monsters from countries that already have one, so each continent always has at least a few monsters to find.
