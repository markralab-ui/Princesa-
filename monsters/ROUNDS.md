# Myth Monster Freeze: the six rounds

All 195 countries of the world (the 193 UN members plus Vatican City and Palestine) have at least one monster from their own myths, legends or folk tales, spread over six rounds: 220 monsters in all. Every round has all six continents, so any round can be played on its own; pick one on the start screen. The world map in the Treasure Book lights up each country whose monster you have frozen, in any round.

Continents with few countries (South America, Oceania, North America) also get extra monsters from countries that already have one, so each continent always has at least three to find. Where a country has little recorded folklore, its monster is a well-known legend, folk-tale animal or festival character tied to it, and the fact in the game says so.
